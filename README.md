# Rochers Salon Delhi

Build a complete, high-end website for a modern hair & beauty salon called "[Rochers]" in [delhi]. It should feel like it was designed by a premium creative agency, not generated by AI. Lively, full of motion, with a "wow" first impression.

PAGES / SECTIONS
1. Home (landing page): full-screen hero with a large editorial headline, a big background image or video, and a "Book Now" button. Then: a short brand story, featured services, a scrolling marquee of service names, team preview, gallery, testimonials, and a final booking CTA.
2. Services & Pricing: services grouped by category (Hair, Color, Skin, Nails, Bridal). Each shows name, short description, duration, and price. Clean, easy to scan.
3. Team: stylist cards with photo, name, specialty. Photo changes or zooms on hover.
4. Gallery: masonry-style before/after and work photos with a lightbox.
5. Booking: a smooth multi-step flow: choose service → choose stylist (or "any") → pick date & time slot → enter name/phone/email → confirmation screen. Show available and booked slots clearly.
6. Contact: address, map, hours, phone, WhatsApp button, Instagram link.
7. FAQ and a minimal, stylish footer.

DESIGN STYLE
- Editorial, luxurious, warm. Think award-winning fashion and lifestyle sites.
- Color palette (no gradients): deep espresso #1E1A17, warm cream #F3EDE4, clay/terracotta accent #C0643F, soft sage #A3AE96. Use the accent sparingly.
- Fonts: mix a stylish serif for headings (Instrument Serif or Fraunces, with some italic words) with a clean sans-serif for body text (Manrope or Inter Tight). Use very large headline sizes.
- Lots of breathing room, asymmetric layouts, big real-looking photography, thin line dividers, rounded buttons.

MOTION & INTERACTION (use Framer Motion)
- Smooth scrolling.
- Headlines reveal word-by-word or line-by-line on load and on scroll.
- Images fade/slide in and have subtle parallax.
- Infinite horizontal marquee for service names.
- Magnetic or animated hover effects on buttons and cards.
- Sticky/stacking cards for the featured services section.
- Smooth page transitions.
- Keep animations elegant and fast, never slow or distracting.

AVOID
- No gradients, purple/blue "tech" colors, glassmorphism, or emojis.
- No generic 3-icon feature grids or template-looking sections.
- No stock clip-art icons. Use minimal line icons only where needed.

TECHNICAL
- Fully responsive and polished on mobile (sticky "Book Now" button on mobile).
- Store bookings in a database so slots get blocked once booked.
- Fast loading, good SEO titles and descriptions.
